# Compliance: what cannot be said

Check **before** delivering. A rejected ad costs production time; repeated rejections cost account health.

## Universal

- **Nothing invented**: figures, testimonials, awards, "voted best", deadlines or results the brand cannot
  back. Missing data goes in as `[DATO A CONFIRMAR: ___]`.
- **Unsupported superlatives** ("the best", "leading", "number one") get disapproved on both platforms and
  do not persuade anyway — replace with the fact that would justify them.
- **False urgency**: dates, seats or stock that do not exist.
- **Ad ↔ landing match**: price, offer and condition identical in both.

## Housing, employment and credit (US & Canada)

Accounts promoting home sale or rental in those markets run under a special category: **no targeting by
age, gender, marital status, parental status or ZIP code** (nor income on Meta), plus restrictions on
advertiser-curated audiences in Demand Gen. Creative must not suggest exclusion of protected groups or
address a demographic profile. Outside those markets the legal restriction does not apply, but demographic
targeting in housing performs worse than intent anyway.

## Beauty and health

Result claims are regulated and are the most common cause of rejection in the category. Explicit
before/after of skin or body is heavily restricted on Meta. No therapeutic or curative promises for a
cosmetic product. Ingredient + function is both safe and effective — it is the pattern that dominates
accounts that perform.

## Finance and credit

Rates, instalments and conditions need the disclosure local regulation requires, and total consistency
with the landing page. "No requirements" or "guaranteed approval" are red flags.

## Exit checklist

- [ ] Characters within limit, count shown
- [ ] No invented data; assumptions labelled
- [ ] No unsupported superlatives, no false urgency
- [ ] Claims allowed for the category
- [ ] Matches the landing page (price, offer, promise)
- [ ] Respects tone, forbidden words and authorised faces
- [ ] Correct format and aspect ratio for the placement
- [ ] Special category: no demographic signals or exclusionary language
